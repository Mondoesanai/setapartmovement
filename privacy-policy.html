Set Apart Movement is a brand operated by Inspiring Websites LLC. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use setapartmovement.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this website you agree to this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Identifiers — such as your first and last name, email address, and mobile phone number, when you submit a form on our site.
  • Communications content — the content of any message or inquiry you send us through a form on our site.
  • Community participation data — if you are issued a Set Apart Movement member number, information associated with that number such as your display name, general city, and participation activity.
  • Technical and usage data — such as device type, browser type, and pages viewed, collected automatically through standard web analytics.

We do not knowingly collect payment card information, government identification numbers, or precise geolocation through this website.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to questions and inquiries you submit to us.
  • Send community updates, event announcements, and event reminders to people who have asked to receive them.
  • Operate and maintain community features of the website, such as member numbers and participation standings.
  • Maintain the security and proper functioning of our website.
  • Comply with applicable law and respond to lawful requests.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share the categories of information described above only as follows:

  • Service providers — vendors who help us operate the website and deliver messages on our behalf, such as our hosting provider, database provider, and messaging platform provider. These vendors may only use the information to perform services for us.
  • Legal and safety — when necessary to satisfy any applicable law, regulation, or governmental request, to avoid liability, or to protect our rights, property, or the safety of others.

All of the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.
