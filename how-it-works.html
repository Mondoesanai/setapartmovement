Set Apart Movement
HOW ITWORKS.
Show up consistently. Earn your place. The rewards are real and so is the work.
1
First Step
Show up to your first meetup.
Every Set Apart meetup is a mission. Come ready to move, to serve, and to meet people who are actually trying to do something with their life. No spectators. No sitting around. You’re here to go.
2–3
Milestone
Earn your bracelet and your number.
After attending two to three meetups, you’re recognized as part of the core. You receive your Set Apart bracelet and your permanent number — one of only 100 in existence. No one else will ever hold your number.
NFC
The Bracelet
Your bracelet is NFC-enabled. When anyone taps it with their phone, it opens your personal link. Every tap counts toward your ranking. Wear it. Let people tap it.
● Your bracelet & your number. Forever.
5
Milestone
Claim your shirt. Unlock The Circle.
Five meetups means you’re not just attending — you’re built into the fabric of this thing. At five meetups you earn your official Set Apart shirt and become eligible for an invitation into The Circle, the inner Set Apart community.
● Official Set Apart shirt
● Eligible for The Circle
∞
Always
Invite others. Climb the rankings.
Every time someone taps your bracelet, your tap count goes up. Rankings are tracked publicly and updated in real time. The top members are the ones who are actually growing this thing. Be the reason someone shows up.
● Live ranking on the leaderboard
How Rankings Work
Your rank is based on your total tap count — how many times people have tapped your bracelet. After each meetup, RSVP conversions are updated manually by the movement leader. Rankings refresh live.
