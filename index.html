About Set Apart Movement

A student-led movement built around faith, community, leadership, service, and real impact.

Our mission is to help students discover who God created them to be, build authentic friendships, serve others, and live lives that are truly set apart. We believe students don't have to wait until they're older to make a difference. Every student has influence, purpose, and the ability to impact the people around them right now.

Every meetup is centered around a different mission or challenge. Some meetups focus on serving the community, others focus on leadership, generosity, prayer, or outreach. No matter the theme, every meetup is designed to push students out of their comfort zones, help them grow in their faith, and remind them that following Jesus is something we live — not just something we talk about.

Our goal isn't simply to host events. Our goal is to build a genuine community of students who encourage one another, challenge one another, serve together, and become the generation God has called them to be.

01  ·  Faith

Discover who you are.

Every meetup is rooted in the belief that you were created on purpose, for a purpose. We help students grow in their faith and discover who God made them to be — not in performance, popularity, or pressure.

02  ·  Community

Build real friendships.

Real community forms when people pursue something greater than themselves together. When you serve alongside someone, you leave as more than strangers — you leave as people who did something that mattered.

03  ·  Impact

Go make a difference.

Students don't have to wait. Every student has influence and the ability to impact the people around them right now. We show up, we serve, and we leave every place better than we found it.

Our Vision

We believe this generation has incredible potential. Instead of blending in with culture, we want to challenge students to live differently — to become young men and women of integrity, courage, compassion, leadership, and faith.

Our hope is that every meetup inspires students to take what they learn and live it out in their homes, schools, churches, teams, workplaces, and communities.

This isn't just about attending an event. It's about becoming the kind of person who leaves every place better than they found it.
