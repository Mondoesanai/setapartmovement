What Is Set Apart Movement?

A student-led movement built around faith, community, and real impact. Every meetup is a mission.

01  ·  Faith

Discover who you are.

Every meetup is rooted in the belief that you were created on purpose, for a purpose.

02  ·  Community

Build real friendships.

Real community forms when people pursue something greater than themselves together.

03  ·  Impact

Go make a difference.

Every student has influence right now. We show up, serve, and leave every place better.

What to Expect at Set Apart Movement

  • A hands-on mission challenge — active, not just sitting in a room
  • Real community — people your age, on purpose together
  • Food and drinks after the challenge
  • No pressure, no performance — just show up as you are
